Ecosystem-wide interim licensing

Etalon, QualiaDB and other constituent projects in the ecosystem being developed share an interim licensing approach while the intended licensing and rights fabric and its supporting systems are developed. Each project's own licence notice identifies the applicable variant, version and covered material. Etalon-specific work currently uses CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

See Licensing and rights fabric: purpose, development status and interim licensing for the shared rationale. The intended framework supports cooperative and collaborative work, humanitarian ICT, contributor compensation and accountable licence transitions. Its operational use depends on semantic and logic tooling, rights safeguards, contribution and settlement systems, and governance processes that remain in development.

Adoption requires readiness for the defined use and an authorised decision for identified material. A compensation-based licence transition also requires full satisfaction of its defined agreed obligations. Infrastructure readiness and settlement are distinct requirements; neither automatically changes a licence.

The licence attached to each release and the applicable component notices govern its use. This statement of future intent does not amend those terms or make existing grants expire. Third-party and inherited material retains its own terms and provenance.

Project-specific work

Etalon is an independent project founded and maintained by Timothy Charles Holborn. Development began on 20 October 2026 from a fork of a specific wgpu source snapshot. Subsequent work includes both improvements to inherited functions and new libraries and systems outside the original wgpu codebase. These Etalon contributions have their own authorship and development history and are governed by Etalon's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence. The MIT source reference does not relicense Etalon-specific work.

See the repository's LICENSE and LICENSE-QUALIA. These notices do not replace rights or conditions attached to upstream material.

Inherited graphics implementation

The starting snapshot declared wgpu version 30.0.0 at revision e084b66aab76, and was forked under its MIT licence. Some graphics, validation and shader-compiler foundations, including Naga, originated there. The snapshot offered MIT or Apache-2.0 terms; the repository preserves both upstream texts and attribution.

Reference-only source

Public SDK/interface sources are retained for ABI and provenance review under their individual GPL, BSD or Apache/MIT terms. See the component notices and provenance manifests. Those reference sources are not included in this documentation artifact or compiled into the portable sensing libraries.

Native runtime combinations

Vendor adapters are a separate workspace and require explicit runtime admission. This separation limits accidental dependencies; it is not a legal certification of every runtime combination. In particular, the RTL-SDR adapter dynamically loads GPL-licensed librtlsdr. Dynamic loading alone does not settle the distribution requirements of a combined product.

No native driver, proprietary SDK, firmware or vendor runtime binary is installed or bundled by this site. Review the exact selected SDK/runtime, its dependency closure and redistribution terms before distributing a product that uses it.

Validation and hardware

API documentation and successful software checks do not authenticate a physical device or qualify its scientific accuracy. Native allocation bounds, numerical behavior, reset/cancellation and sample timing need evidence for the particular platform and deployment. Keep unqualified capabilities distinct from measured results.

These boundaries preserve the current project policy. Broad dependency replacement and relicensing are separate work.
