The browser test page runs actual Rust/WASM operations and reports each result: pass, fail or unsupported. It exercises compute, readback, validation and rendering, plus portable NQuin and Forge functions. Results stay on the device unless you download and share them.

What comes from wgpu, and what Etalon adds

The safe graphics API, native backend foundation and shader compiler originate in wgpu/Naga. Etalon retains that attribution and adds its own integration libraries and recorded fixes. See the source-backed changes; browser tests do not establish native driver or WebGPU conformance certification.
