# Upstream review, 30 October 2026

This ledger covers every commit in upstream `gfx-rs/wgpu` after common ancestor
`e084b66aab767940ff1236604d194c9e1c2844b0` through
`49325ca0a13bdc0787b1601138096cce743dd29e`: 62 commits. The review compares the
actual upstream patches with the renamed, extended Etalon working tree, rather
than treating commit titles or matching dates as evidence of applicability.

**Source implementation is not validation acceptance.** The ports and regression
fixtures below are present in the working tree. Their checks are planned at the
supervisor's combined validation gate; this ledger claims no passing builds,
tests, platform execution, browser execution or CTS run. Earlier formatting and
static-DXC build failures remain failures to investigate in the validation log.
Dependency resolution remains a separate gate.

“Applied” includes mechanical `wgpu` to `etalon` path/name adaptation. “Retained”
means an intentional fork compatibility decision, not an upstream defect being
ignored without review. “Retired” means the affected runner or binding is removed
from this fork; it does not establish equivalent coverage in the new harness.
No independent normative WebGPU/WGSL section verification is asserted here, so
this ledger adds no unverified specification anchors. The linked upstream diffs
are primary evidence for the changes being reviewed.
